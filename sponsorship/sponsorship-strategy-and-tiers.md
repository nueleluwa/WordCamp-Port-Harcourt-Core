This is the lookup document — for the outreach process, see [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md); for what happens after a sponsor says yes, see [sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md).

**How these benefits work:**
- Complimentary tickets are for the sponsor's own staff, not giveaways.
- Discount codes and offers are used by attendees by choice. No attendee data is shared with sponsors.
- Raffles are run by organizers. Sponsors donate the prize and do not receive entries.
- Sponsors collect leads themselves at their booth, with the attendee's consent.
- Impact report numbers are aggregate only, with no personal data.

> **To confirm:** ticket counts and which tier gets each benefit are proposed. The Sponsorship Lead should confirm them with Finance before they go into the sponsorship deck.

**Hard rule, not a guideline:** no tier includes speaking time. Speaking slots are never sold, guaranteed, or influenced by sponsorship, and must not appear in any tier, deck, outreach email, or agreement — see [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md). If the Program team separately wants a sponsor's representative to speak on a particular topic, that is a Program team decision, kept apart from the sponsorship and reviewed like any other talk.

> **Open decision:** category exclusivity between competing sponsors is not yet defined — see [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md).

## Pipeline stages

Sponsors move through a single pipeline, tracked on the Sponsorship dashboard (see [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md)):

1. **Prospect** — identified, not yet contacted
2. **Proposal Sent** — initial outreach sent
3. **In Discussion** — responded, negotiating
4. **Confirmed** — committed, invoice sent/paid

## Sponsorship KPIs

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Prospects Identified | 100 | Database count |
| Contact Rate | 80% | Contacted / Prospects |
| Response Rate | 30% | Responded / Contacted |
| Conversion Rate | 15% | Confirmed / Prospects |
| Average Deal Size | ₦1,000,000 | Revenue / Confirmed |
| Time to Close | 30 days | Days from contact to confirmed |
