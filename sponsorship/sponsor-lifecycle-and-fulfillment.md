# Sponsor Lifecycle & Fulfillment

**Purpose:** Everything that happens after a sponsor confirms, through the event, to the post-event thank-you. Getting a "yes" is the start of the sponsor relationship, not the end of the task.

**Owner:** Sponsorship Lead

## From confirmation to event day

Triggered by sending Template 4 in [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md):

1. Invoice sent with bank transfer details — see [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md)
2. Payment received and reconciled
3. High-resolution logo collected (PNG or SVG)
4. Sponsor contact person confirmed for ongoing communications
5. Booth specifications sent (if the tier includes booth space) — coordinate with Logistics, see [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](../venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md)
6. Sponsor page blurb (50–100 words) and link collected, or name and link only for Community (hand off to Marketing — [marketing-and-communications/](../marketing-and-communications/))
7. Logo added to website, signage, and screens between sessions, and to the tier-specific placements (backdrop, volunteer T-shirts, lanyards, printed schedule, photo-booth frame). Collect the print-ready logo before the printing deadline.
8. Names of sponsor staff collected and complimentary tickets issued (tickets are for the sponsor's own staff, not giveaways)
9. Swag bag item or flyer received by the deadline and counted (coordinate with Logistics)
10. Discount code or offer and its terms collected, to go in the swag bag or on the sponsor page (attendees opt in; no attendee data is shared)
11. Raffle prize confirmed and delivered, if the sponsor is donating one (organizers run the raffle; the sponsor does not receive entries)
12. Non-program item chosen and confirmed with Logistics — lunch, coffee breaks, charging station, Wi-Fi card, or water station (Platinum chooses first, then Gold)
13. Hiring board details collected (Platinum and Gold)
14. Sponsor profile article scheduled with Marketing (Platinum and Gold)
15. Networking lunch or coffee invitation sent and booth photo shot list agreed (Platinum and Gold)
16. Student ticket funding recorded and "Student Champion" badge confirmed, if the sponsor is funding student tickets

Not every step applies to every tier — check [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md) "Benefits by tier" and skip what the sponsor's tier does not include.

Track each step's completion on the sponsor's dashboard record, not as a side checklist — see [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md).

## On event day

Sponsor booths are staffed and monitored as part of the event-day runbook — see [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook.md) "Sponsor Booth Area." Any sponsor-reported issue during the event routes through the same escalation path as any other event-day issue — see [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook.md).

Sponsor benefits delivered on the day: the MC gives a brief thank-you (Platinum and Gold, no speaking time), the networking lunch or coffee runs, the photographer captures the booth photos, and the hiring board is set up.

## Post-event: sponsor thank-you (within 48 hours)

Send the impact report (aggregate numbers only) and the thank-you plaque or certificate for the sponsor's tier with this email. Also confirm the recap article, newsletter, and recap video logo credits went out for tiers that include them.

**Subject:** Thank You for Supporting WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]

> Hello [Contact Name],
>
> On behalf of the entire WordCamp Port Harcourt organizing team, I want to extend our heartfelt thanks to [Company Name] for being a [Sponsor Tier] sponsor of WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year].
>
> **EVENT HIGHLIGHTS:**
> - [X] attendees from across Nigeria's South-South region
> - [X] speakers sharing knowledge on WordPress development, design, and business
> - [X] workshops and hands-on sessions
> - Countless connections made between developers, agencies, and businesses
>
> Your support made this possible. We've attached a sponsor impact report with photos and metrics from the event.
>
> We hope you found value in the partnership and would love to have [Company Name] sponsor again next year. We'll reach out in [Month] when we begin planning WordCamp Port Harcourt [Next Year].
>
> Thank you again for believing in the WordPress community.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Your Name]
> Sponsorship Team Lead, WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]
> [Your Email] | [Your Phone]
>
> P.S. We've tagged [Company Name] in our social media posts from the event. Feel free to share!

This is one line item within the broader post-event workflow — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow.md).

## Feeding next year's pipeline

At post-event review (2 weeks out), update the sponsor's dashboard record with renewal likelihood and notes for next year's Sponsorship Lead — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow.md) "Documentation Updates." Do not let this context live only in one organizer's memory. Note which sponsors are owed the first option to renew (Platinum and Gold) or a locked-in price for a two-year commitment (Platinum, Gold, and Silver), so the next Sponsorship Lead honors it.
